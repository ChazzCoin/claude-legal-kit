# Vocabulary — Jurisdictions

Courts and jurisdictions the firm works in. Tag format: kebab-case.

The kit ships **Alabama** as its worked-example jurisdiction, paired with `firm/practice-kit/shared-library/jurisdictions/alabama-state/`. A firm in another state keeps the federal and neutral tags below and replaces the sample-jurisdiction section with its own state's courts.

## Federal

- **federal** — umbrella tag for federal courts — *added 2026-05-15*
- **scotus** — Supreme Court of the United States — *added 2026-05-15*
- **11th-circuit** — U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit — *added 2026-05-15*

Add the circuit(s) the firm practices in, format `<N>th-circuit`. Federal district-court tags are listed in the sample-jurisdiction section below, format `<state-initials><district>`.

## Sample state jurisdiction — Alabama

The kit's worked example. A firm elsewhere replaces this whole section with its own state's courts.

- **alabama** — umbrella tag for Alabama state courts and law — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-supreme** — Alabama Supreme Court — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-civil-appeals** — Alabama Court of Civil Appeals — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-criminal-appeals** — Alabama Court of Criminal Appeals — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-jefferson** — Jefferson County Circuit Court (Birmingham) — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-shelby** — Shelby County Circuit Court — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-madison** — Madison County Circuit Court (Huntsville) — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-mobile** — Mobile County Circuit Court — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-montgomery** — Montgomery County Circuit Court — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-tuscaloosa** — Tuscaloosa County Circuit Court — *added 2026-05-15*
- **al-other-county** — any other Alabama county not yet listed — *added 2026-05-15*
- **ndal** — U.S. District Court, Northern District of Alabama — *added 2026-05-15*
- **mdal** — U.S. District Court, Middle District of Alabama — *added 2026-05-15*
- **sdal** — U.S. District Court, Southern District of Alabama — *added 2026-05-15*

State trial courts follow `<state>-<county>`; federal district courts follow `<state-initials><district>` (e.g. `ndal`).

## Jurisdiction-neutral

- **general** — applies to any jurisdiction (trial-skills content, persuasion theory, general doctrine) — *added 2026-05-15*
- **other-state** — umbrella tag for non-resident state authority being referenced — *added 2026-05-15*
