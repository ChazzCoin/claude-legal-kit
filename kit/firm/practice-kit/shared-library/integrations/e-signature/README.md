## Confidentiality notes

- E-signature services see the document content during signing. Both DocuSign and AdobeSign meet the standards for privileged documents — they treat content as confidential under their professional-service tiers.
- Confirm the firm's account is on a tier with the right confidentiality protections before sending anything privileged.
