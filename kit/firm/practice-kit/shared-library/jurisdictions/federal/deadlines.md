# Federal — deadlines

Deadline rules for U.S. federal court — the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, which apply nationwide. The firm-level method is in `firm/practice-kit/shared-library/rules/deadlines.md`; this file supplies the federal numbers. A district's **local rules** add to these — keep those in that federal district's own jurisdiction folder.

**Verify against the current Rules — these are working defaults, not a substitute for checking.**

## Time computation — FRCP 6

- The day of the triggering event is **not** counted; the last day **is** counted.
- Weekends and legal holidays at the end of a period extend it to the next business day.
- **All intermediate days count** — the post-2009 amendments removed the old exclusion of weekends in short periods.
- Service by mail, or electronic service after 5:00 PM local court time — add **3 days** (FRCP 6(d)).

## Procedural deadlines — most common

| Event | Deadline | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Answer to complaint | 21 days after service | FRCP 12(a)(1)(A) |
| Answer (waiver of service used) | 60 days after waiver sent | FRCP 4(d)(3) |
| Response to interrogatories | 30 days after service | FRCP 33(b)(2) |
| Response to RFPs | 30 days after service | FRCP 34(b)(2)(A) |
| Response to RFAs | 30 days after service | FRCP 36(a)(3) |
| Initial expert disclosures | 90 days before trial (default) | FRCP 26(a)(2)(D) |
| Rebuttal expert disclosures | 30 days after the other side's | FRCP 26(a)(2)(D)(ii) |
| Pretrial disclosures (witness / exhibit) | 30 days before trial | FRCP 26(a)(3)(B) |
